Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What documents are required for admission?
A: 10th and 12th mark sheets, transfer certificate, JEE/CET scorecard, 
   category certificate (if applicable), passport-size photos, Aadhar card.

Q: What is the last date to apply?
A: Applications close on 30th June every year. Late applications may be 
   accepted with a penalty fee until 10th July.

Q: Is there a management quota?
A: Yes, 15% of seats are reserved under management quota with separate 
   fee structure.
