Eligibility Criteria for B.Tech Admission

- Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Minimum aggregate of 60% marks (55% for reserved categories).
- Valid score in JEE Main or the respective state CET is mandatory.
- Age limit: Candidate must not be older than 21 years as of admission year 
  (relaxation for reserved categories as per government norms).
